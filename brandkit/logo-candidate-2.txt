Abstract flat vector symbol for a quiet voice-capture utility. One solid circle divided into three crescent slivers of decreasing width by two curved cuts that are offset laterally rather than concentric, so the shapes read as a sound travelling sideways and thinning rather than broadcasting outward in rings. Construction is strictly circular: a single master circle, two arc subtractions sharing the circle's radius but displaced along the horizontal axis, the widest crescent at the left and the narrowest at the right, the gaps between them equal and carrying as much visual weight as the solids. Style register is disciplined, geometric and quietly technical. Strict two colour use: the locked accent fills the crescents, the locked background shows through the cuts, with no third colour introduced. Centred, isolated, generous even margin, mass distributed so the mark holds together when reduced to a small interface glyph. Flat vector design, clean lines, no shadows, no texture, no text. Clean editable vector paths, SVG-friendly, minimal anchor points.
