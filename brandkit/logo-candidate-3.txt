Abstract flat vector symbol for a quiet voice-capture utility. One horizontal rounded bar with a single smooth U-shaped notch cut upward into its lower edge and placed off centre, so the removed shape reads at once as a dip in a level meter and as the strip of interface the tool inhabits. Construction is a rectangle with fully rounded end caps, its height about one fifth of its width, the notch a half ellipse roughly one third of the bar's length positioned left of centre, its curve meeting the bar's underside tangentially so no sharp corner is left. Style register is spare, functional and confident, an interface component rather than a decorative badge. Strict two colour use: the locked accent fills the bar, the locked background occupies the notch, with no third colour introduced. Centred, isolated, generous even margin, the silhouette wide and stable and legible when reduced to a small interface glyph. Flat vector design, clean lines, no shadows, no texture, no text. Clean editable vector paths, SVG-friendly, minimal anchor points.
