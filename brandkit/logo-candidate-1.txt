Abstract monoline symbol for a quiet voice-capture utility. One continuous open stroke that curves inward through roughly three quarters of a turn and terminates in a single solid round dot resting inside the curve's own opening, so the enclosed negative space reads as a held breath. Construction is a single arc of constant radius with rounded caps, the dot placed off centre toward the lower right, the whole silhouette circular and balanced with deliberate asymmetry in where the stroke opens. Style register is calm, contemporary and utilitarian, closer to a precise instrument than a friendly consumer badge. Strict two colour use: the locked accent carries the stroke and the dot, the locked background sits behind as a solid field, with no third colour introduced. Centred, isolated, generous even margin, proportioned to stay legible when reduced to a small interface glyph. Monoline vector design, uniform stroke weight, rounded line caps, no fills, no shadows, no texture, no text. Clean editable vector paths, SVG-friendly, minimal anchor points.
